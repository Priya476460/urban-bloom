Delivery & FAQ

Everything you need to know about delivery, collection and ordering.

Delivery Areas

We deliver across Central Auckland and nearby suburbs, including:

  • Ponsonby and Grey Lynn
  • Parnell and Newmarket
  • Mount Eden and Epsom
  • Remuera

Other Auckland areas may be possible by arrangement.

Good to Know

  • Delivery runs Monday to Saturday
  • Please allow at least one working day's notice
  • Same-day requests may be possible but aren't guaranteed
  • Delivery charges depend on location
  • Weddings, events and large orders need extra notice
  • Collection from our shop can be arranged

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I request particular flowers?

Yes. You can request particular flowers or colours, but availability depends on the season and current supply. We'll suggest suitable alternatives if needed.

Do you offer same-day delivery?

Same-day delivery may be available for some orders placed early in the day, but it can't be guaranteed.

Can you cater for dietary requirements with gift add-ons?

Please mention any requirements in your enquiry. Availability of suitable gift items may vary.

How much notice do you need for weddings or events?

At least three weeks for smaller events, and considerably earlier for weddings or large events.

Do you provide regular flowers for businesses?

Yes. Weekly, fortnightly and other recurring arrangements can be discussed with Urban Bloom.

Can I order and pay on the website?

The website is for browsing and enquiries. Online payment isn't currently available.
